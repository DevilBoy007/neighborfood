Delete your Neighborfood account

Neighborfood, LLC

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Neighborfood and sign in.
  2. Tap your profile picture to open Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account, enter your password, and type DELETE.

Finish or cancel any open orders first. Your account is deleted immediately.

Without the app

Email dylan@bakr.dev from the address on your account with the subject "Delete my account". We will verify the request and delete your account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, contact details, and location
  • Your shops, item listings, and photos
  • Your message threads and blocked-user list
  • Your saved payment methods

What is kept

Order and payment records are kept for up to seven years for accounting, tax, and dispute purposes, with your phone number, delivery address, and order notes removed. Reports about policy violations may be kept to protect the community. Active subscriptions must be cancelled separately in your App Store or Google Play settings.
