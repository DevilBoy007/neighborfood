Privacy Policy

Effective October 31, 2026

Neighborfood, LLC ("Neighborfood", "we", "us") operates the Neighborfood mobile app and website (the "Service"), a local marketplace where neighbors buy, sell, and trade home-grown and homemade food. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Account details: first and last name, email address, username, password (stored by Firebase Authentication, never by us in plain text), phone number, date of birth, and profile photo.
  • Address and location: the street address, city, state, and ZIP code you enter, and its map coordinates.
  • Marketplace content: shops, item listings, photos, prices, trade offers, messages you send to other users, and reports you submit.
  • Orders: items purchased or sold, delivery or pickup choice, delivery address, contact phone, special instructions, and order history.
  • Support requests: anything you send us through Contact Us or email.

Information collected automatically

  • Device location: with your permission, and only while you use the app, we read your device's approximate location to show nearby shops and set your local market. You can turn this off in your device settings.
  • Push notification token: if you allow notifications, a device token so we can alert you about messages and order updates.
  • Diagnostics: basic technical information such as app version, device type, and error logs used to keep the Service working.

Information from partners

  • Payments (Stripe): card payments, saved payment methods, and seller payouts are handled by Stripe, Inc. We receive limited details such as the card brand, last four digits, and expiration date, plus payment and payout status. We never see or store full card numbers. Sellers provide identity and bank details directly to Stripe for verification and payouts.
  • Subscriptions (Apple, Google, RevenueCat): if you subscribe to Neighborfood Pro, Apple or Google processes the purchase and we receive your subscription status through RevenueCat, Inc. We do not receive your store payment details.

2. How we use information

  • To create and secure your account and provide the Service.
  • To show shops and items near you and group users into local markets.
  • To process orders, hold payments in escrow, release funds to sellers, issue refunds, and record trades.
  • To let buyers and sellers message each other and to send notifications.
  • To provide Neighborfood Pro features to subscribers.
  • To keep the community safe: reviewing reports, enforcing our Terms and Community Guidelines, and preventing fraud and abuse.
  • To respond to support requests and to comply with legal and tax obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

3. How information is shared

  • With other users: your username, first name, profile photo, shops, and listings are visible to other signed-in users. When you place an order, the seller sees the order details plus the contact phone and delivery address you provide so they can fulfil it. Messages are visible to the people in the conversation.
  • With service providers who process data for us under contract: Google (Firebase, Cloud Functions, Firestore, Cloud Storage, Google Maps and Places), Stripe (payments and payouts), RevenueCat, Apple and Google (subscriptions and push notifications), and Expo (push notification delivery and app updates).
  • For legal reasons: when required by law, to respond to valid legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of our users, the public, or Neighborfood.
  • Business transfers: as part of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, in which case this policy will continue to apply to your information.

4. Retention

We keep your information while your account is active. When you delete your account we permanently delete your profile, shops, listings, photos, and message threads. We keep order and payment records for up to seven years to meet accounting, tax, and dispute-resolution obligations, with your phone number, delivery address, and order notes removed. Reports you submit or that concern you may be kept to protect the community. Stripe retains payment records under its own legal obligations.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Access and correction: view and edit your profile in Settings > Edit Details.
  • Deletion: delete your account at any time in Settings > Delete Account, or email us.
  • Location and notifications: manage these permissions in your device settings.
  • Blocking: block other users from Settings > Blocked Users or from any report screen.

Depending on where you live, including under the Indiana Consumer Data Protection Act and the California Consumer Privacy Act, you may have the right to confirm whether we process your data, access it, correct it, delete it, obtain a portable copy, and opt out of sale, targeted advertising, or profiling (we do none of these). To exercise a right, email dylan@bakr.dev. We will verify your request and reply within 45 days. If we decline, you may appeal by replying to our decision, and we will respond within 60 days. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

6. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards, including encrypted connections, server-side access controls, and payment processing by PCI-compliant Stripe. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

7. Children

The Service is intended for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Where data is processed

Neighborfood is based in the United States and your information is processed and stored in the United States.

9. Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date. If changes are material, we will notify you in the app or by email before they take effect.
