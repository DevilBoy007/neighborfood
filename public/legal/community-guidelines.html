Community Guidelines

Effective October 31, 2026

Neighborfood works because neighbors trust each other. These guidelines are part of our Terms of Service, and breaking them can lead to content removal or account suspension.

Be a good neighbor

  • Be respectful. No harassment, hate speech, threats, or bullying.
  • Describe items honestly, with accurate photos, quantities, prices, and allergens.
  • Show up for pickups and deliveries you agree to, or cancel early.
  • Keep payments in the app so escrow and refunds protect everyone.

Food safety

  • Follow your state's cottage food laws and any permit or labeling requirements.
  • Handle, store, and transport food safely, and label common allergens.
  • Never list expired, spoiled, recalled, or contaminated food.

Prohibited items

  • Alcohol, tobacco, nicotine, cannabis or hemp-derived THC, and other drugs.
  • Foods your state does not allow to be sold from a home kitchen, which may include raw or unpasteurized milk, meat, seafood, or foods needing refrigeration.
  • Live animals, weapons, hazardous materials, and anything illegal to sell.
  • Counterfeit goods or anything you don't have the right to sell or trade.

Prohibited content

  • Sexually explicit, violent, or graphic content.
  • Spam, scams, phishing links, or misleading listings.
  • Other people's personal information shared without consent.

Reporting and blocking

Tap the flag icon on any shop, item, conversation, or order, or use Menu > report a problem. You can also block a user so you won't see their shops or messages. Our team reviews every report within 24 hours. For emergencies, contact local authorities first.
